FEATURES

  • Hand tracking: 21 landmarks, one or two hands, skeleton view.
  • Air Mouse: monkey or dot cursor, pinch click, hold to drag, double pinch, fist pause, calibration.
  • Air Touch: taps, long presses and drags on the real screen.
  • Air Scroll and navigation: scroll, back, home, recents.
  • Air Keyboard: floating keyboard, never types into password fields. Invisible air typing is an experiment.
  • Command Wheel: home, back, recents, screenshot, volume, brightness, music, keyboard, flashlight and your apps.
  • Gesture Studio + Air Drawing: record your own shapes and assign actions.
  • HUD and Practice mode: a safe coach where nothing real is triggered.
  • Voice commands: optional, on-device only.

INSTALL

  1. Tap DOWNLOAD APK and open the file.
  2. Allow installs from your browser if Android asks.
  3. Open PhantomTouch. Fix every ✕ row on the dashboard.
  4. Set Battery to Unrestricted for the app.
  5. Tap CALIBRATE once, then PRACTICE.
  6. Tap START AIR TOUCH while the app is open.

PROBLEMS AND FIXES

  • Play Protect "App blocked": Play Store, profile, Play Protect, gear, turn off scanning, install, turn it back on.
  • Accessibility switch greyed out: Settings, Apps, PhantomTouch, ⋮ menu, Allow restricted settings.
  • After an update: turn the Accessibility service off and on.
  • "App not installed" when updating: uninstall the old version first.
  • Permission popups do not show: other overlay apps can block them. Stop those apps, or grant Camera in Settings.
  • Camera stops with the screen off: set Battery to Unrestricted.
  • Demo camera blocked: tap the lock icon next to the address, Permissions, Camera, Allow.

HONEST LIMITS

Needs good light and your whole hand in view. Expect some jitter, so big buttons work best. Secure screens (banking, some system dialogs) cannot be controlled. This is a test build, so it is not on the Play Store.
